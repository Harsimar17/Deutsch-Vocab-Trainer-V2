      Use Talkloop in ChatGPT

      ChatGPT can be your partner instead, with no API key: Talkloop's screen opens inside ChatGPT, with your words to say today, corrections and the microphone, and your mistakes and words are saved here.

      1. In ChatGPT on a computer: Settings → Plugins (or Apps) → Add → Add custom MCP server. If you don't see Add, turn on Developer mode under Advanced settings first.
      2. Name it Talkloop, paste the address below, Authentication None, and save.
      3. In a new chat, turn Talkloop on and write e.g. Talkloop: interview me, A2 or Talkloop: Konnektoren mit weil, A2. Tap 🎙️ on the Talkloop screen and speak.

      This address works like your login for Talkloop for 180 days: don't share it.
